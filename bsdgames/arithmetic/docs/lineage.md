# `arithmetic` — Lineage

`arithmetic` is one of the earliest examples of computer-assisted
instruction in the BSDGames collection.

---

## Ancestors & Siblings

- **Paper flash cards** — the mechanical predecessor.
- **`bc(1)` / `dc(1)`** — Unix calculator tools that perform the same
  operations non-interactively.
- **`quiz`** — another BSDGames educational drill, but data-driven.

## Modern Descendants

- **Khan Academy math exercises** — adaptive problem sets with hints.
- **Anki / SuperMemo** — spaced-repetition systems that also bias
  toward missed items.
- **Math-drill mobile apps** — timed, gamified arithmetic practice.
- **Classroom clicker systems** — real-time competitive quizzes.

## Genre Notes

`arithmetic` is a **drill / educational game**. It is grouped with
`quiz` in the BSDGames Puzzle & Word Games category.
