# `factor` — Lineage

> Where `factor` sits in the family tree of mathematical utilities.

---

## Direct Siblings (BSDGames)

- `number` — spells out numbers in English; another small text utility.
- `random` — random line filter; shares the "tiny filter" design philosophy.
- `caesar` / `rot13` — simple text transformations.

## Genre Ancestors

- Classic UNIX `bc`, `dc` — arbitrary-precision calculators.
- `primes` utilities from various Unix systems — generate or test primes.

## Modern Descendants

- Online prime-factor calculators (WolframAlpha, factorization.info).
- Educational programming exercises (Project Euler, Rosetta Code).
- Cryptographic key-size demonstrators.
- Browser-based factor-tree visualisations.

## Influence

`factor` exemplifies the UNIX philosophy of doing one thing well. Its influence is visible in every small command-line math tool that values speed and simplicity over features.
