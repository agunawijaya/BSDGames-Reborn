# `number` — Lineage

> Where `number` sits in the family tree of text utilities.

---

## Direct Siblings (BSDGames)

- `factor` — another small mathematical utility.
- `random` — random line filter; shares the "tiny filter" design philosophy.
- `caesar` / `rot13` — simple text transformations.

## Genre Ancestors

- Classic UNIX text filters (`sed`, `awk`, `tr`).
- `spell` and other linguistic tools shipped with early BSD.

## Modern Descendants

- Cheque-printing and invoicing libraries.
- Browser-based "number to words" converters.
- Accounting software localisation modules.
- Programming exercise sites (Rosetta Code, LeetCode).

## Influence

`number` is a compact example of natural-language generation from structured data. Its influence appears wherever software must produce human-readable amounts or quantities.
