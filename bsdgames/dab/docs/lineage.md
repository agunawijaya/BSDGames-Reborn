# `dab` — Lineage

`dab` is a digital version of the pencil-and-paper game *Dots and
Boxes*.

---

## Ancestors & Siblings

- **Dots and Boxes** — the pencil-and-paper original (also known as
  *Boxes*, *Squares*, or *Pigs in a Pen*).
- **Other terminal board games** in BSDGames such as `gomoku`.

## Modern Descendants

- **Mobile Dots and Boxes apps** — single-player and online
  multiplayer.
- **Browser implementations** — HTML5 canvas / SVG versions.
- **AI research** — combinatorial game theory work by Berlekamp and
  others.

## Genre Notes

`dab` is a **board game / combinatorial game**. It is grouped with
`gomoku`, `backgammon`, and `monop` in the BSDGames Board Games
category.
