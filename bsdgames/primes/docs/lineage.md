# `primes` — Lineage

> Where `primes` sits in the family tree of mathematical utilities.

---

## Direct Siblings (BSDGames)

- `factor` — factor integers into primes; same author and prime table.
- `number` — convert numbers to English words.
- `random` — probabilistic line filter.

## Genre Ancestors

- Sieve of Eratosthenes — ancient algorithm.
- UNIX prime utilities and `bc`/`dc` arbitrary-precision calculators.

## Modern Descendants

- Online prime generators and calculators.
- Cryptographic prime generators (much larger scale).
- Educational tools that visualise the Sieve of Eratosthenes.
- Programming exercise sites (Project Euler, Rosetta Code).

## Influence

`primes` is the practical sibling of `factor` in BSD mathematics utilities. It influenced every later command-line tool that needs a fast, bounded prime list.
