# `pom` — Lineage

`pom` belongs to the Unix family of scientific and reference utilities
that put serious algorithms behind simple interfaces.

---

## Ancestors & Siblings

- **`date(1)`** — shares the compressed date/time format.
- **Astronomical algorithms by Peter Duffett-Smith** — the source of
  the calculation.
- **`xphoon` / `wmoon` / `kcmoon`** — X11 and desktop moon-phase
  applets.

## Modern Descendants

- **Moon phase widgets** on phones, watches, and desktops.
- **Astronomy apps** such as Stellarium, SkySafari, etc.
- **Web APIs** returning lunar illumination for any date.
- **Astrology and gardening apps** that track moon phases.

## Genre Notes

`pom` is a **reference / astronomy utility**. It is grouped with
`fortune`, `countmail`, and `pom` in the BSDGames package.
