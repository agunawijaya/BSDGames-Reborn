# `banner` — Lineage

> Where `banner` sits in the family tree of ASCII art tools.

---

## Direct Siblings (BSDGames)

- `bcd` — punch card format display.
- `ppt` — paper tape format display.
- `rain` / `worms` — curses screensavers.

## Genre Ancestors

- Early line-printer art and teletype banners.
- Typewriter art and ASCII art traditions.

## Modern Descendants

- `figlet` and `toilet` — ASCII/Unicode text banners.
- Online ASCII art generators.
- Retro terminal decoration scripts.
- Sign-making apps and social-media text images.

## Influence

`banner` established the pattern of “type text, get big text” on command-line systems. Its descendants still dominate terminal decoration and meme text generation.
