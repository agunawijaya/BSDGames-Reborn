# `quiz` — Lineage

`quiz` is the BSDGames trivia/drill utility.

---

## Ancestors & Siblings

- **Flash cards** — the mechanical predecessor.
- **`arithmetic`** — another BSDGames drill program.

## Modern Descendants

- Anki, Quizlet, and other flash-card / spaced-repetition apps.
- Trivia bots and quiz games on messaging platforms.
- Educational platforms with reversible question banks.

## Genre Notes

`quiz` is a **drill / educational game**. It is grouped with
`arithmetic` and `boggle` in the BSDGames Puzzle & Word Games
category.
