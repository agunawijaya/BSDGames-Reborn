## Author Biographies

The 1980-1993 authors of `snake` are not individually credited in
the source file I read. The copyright is held by "The Regents of
the University of California." Reasonable candidates from BSD
history: Ken Arnold, Bill Joy, or other Berkeley staff and
students of the period. Not conclusively identified.
