# `fish` — Lineage

`fish` is the BSDGames implementation of the children's card game *Go
Fish*.

---

## Ancestors & Siblings

- **Go Fish** — the traditional card game.
- **`cribbage`** / **`canfield`** — other card games in BSDGames.

## Modern Descendants

- Mobile Go Fish apps.
- Web-based card rooms.
- Casual multiplayer games with similar "ask and draw" mechanics.

## Genre Notes

`fish` is a **card game**. It is grouped with `canfield`, `cribbage`,
and `mille` in the BSDGames Card Games category.
