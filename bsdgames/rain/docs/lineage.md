# `rain` — Lineage

> Where `rain` sits in the family tree of screensavers.

---

## Direct Siblings (BSDGames)

- `worms` — another curses screensaver by Eric P. Scott.
- `banner` — ASCII art generator.
- `gravity` — physics simulation demo (if present in collection).

## Genre Ancestors

- VAX/VMS `rain` — the program that inspired the BSD version.
- Early CRT terminal demos and DEC VT100 screensavers.

## Modern Descendants

- Browser-based ASCII/Canvas rain animations.
- Matrix "digital rain" screensavers.
- Mobile relaxation apps with rain sounds.
- Desktop wallpaper engines with particle rain.

## Influence

`rain` proved that text terminals could deliver soothing, aesthetic motion. It helped establish the tradition of terminal screensavers and ASCII art demos.
