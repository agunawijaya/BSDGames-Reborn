## NAME

**wargames** — shall we play a game?

## SYNOPSIS

**wargames**

## DESCRIPTION

> "Shall we play a game?" -- computer, **wargames**

Just like in the movie, the computer will happily play a game with
you. The likelihood of Global Thermonuclear Warfare resulting is much
smaller....

## SEE ALSO

*Wargames*, the movie (an MGM production, PG 13, directed by John
Badham, 1983).

## Annotations

- The man page is almost entirely a tribute to the film. It lists
  the movie itself in `SEE ALSO`, which is unusual for a Unix manual
  page.
- The script it documents was written earlier, at Berkeley, and
  carries the standard BSD copyright notice.
