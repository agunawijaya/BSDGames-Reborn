# `wargames` — Lineage

`wargames` sits at the intersection of Unix Easter eggs and movie
references.

---

## Direct Ancestors

- BSDGames shell-script utilities
- The 1983 film *WarGames* (MGM, directed by John Badham)

## Genre Siblings

- Other BSDGames Easter eggs and one-shot jokes
- Unix `fortune` (cultural one-liners, though not movie-specific)

## Modern Descendants

- Terminal application launchers (e.g. `fzf`, `rofi`)
- Voice assistants that respond to playful prompts
- Retro-themed game launchers and minigame collections

## Why It Matters

`wargames` proves that a program can be memorable without being
complex. Its entire identity is a prompt, a lookup, and a punchline.
