# `adventure` — Walkthrough

> Definitive dual-walkthrough for *Colossal Cave Adventure* (Woods 350-Point Version).
>
> 1. **Walkthrough A: Shortest Path to Victory** — Fast exploration and exit path.
> 2. **Walkthrough B: The 350-Point Grandmaster Path** — Step-by-step complete solution recovering all 15 treasures, triggering the cave closing, and escaping the repository.

---

## Walkthrough A — Shortest Path to Victory

A quick sprint into Colossal Cave to unlock the grate, claim the gold nugget, and return to the building to establish an honorable retirement.

- **Objective:** Score ~70+ points and retire safely in under 30 turns.
- **Estimated Time:** 2 minutes.

### Step-by-Step Commands

1. `ENTER BUILDING` (Inside building)
2. `TAKE KEYS`
3. `TAKE LAMP`
4. `OUT`
5. `SOUTH` (Valley)
6. `SOUTH` (Slit in rock)
7. `SOUTH` (Grate entrance)
8. `UNLOCK GRATE`
9. `OPEN GRATE`
10. `DOWN` (Below grate)
11. `LIGHT LAMP`
12. `WEST` (Cobble crawl)
13. `WEST` (Debris room)
14. `XYZZY` (Teleport to building)
15. `XYZZY` (Teleport back to Debris room)
16. `WEST` (Sloping canyon)
17. `DOWN` (Bird chamber)
18. `SOUTH` (Top of pit)
19. `EAST` (Hall of Mists)
20. `SOUTH` (Nugget room)
21. `TAKE GOLD`
22. `NORTH` (Hall of Mists)
23. `WEST` (Top of pit)
24. `NORTH` (Bird chamber)
25. `UP` (Sloping canyon)
26. `EAST` (Debris room)
27. `XYZZY` (Teleport inside Building!)
28. `DROP GOLD`
29. `SCORE`
30. `QUIT`

## Walkthrough B — The 350-Point Grandmaster Path

The complete canonical walkthrough to collect all 15 treasures, deposit them in the building, trigger the closing sequence, survive the repository, and earn the Grandmaster title (350 points).

### Phase 1: Surface Preparation & The Crystal Bridge
1. `ENTER BUILDING` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE LAMP` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE KEYS` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE BOTTLE` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE FOOD` $\rightarrow$ `OUT`
2. `SOUTH` $\rightarrow$ `SOUTH` $\rightarrow$ `SOUTH` (Outside grate)
3. `UNLOCK GRATE` $\rightarrow$ `OPEN GRATE` $\rightarrow$ `DOWN`
4. `LIGHT LAMP` $\rightarrow$ `WEST` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE CAGE` $\rightarrow$ `WEST` (Debris room)
5. `XYZZY` (Verify teleport connection) $\rightarrow$ `XYZZY`
6. `WEST` $\rightarrow$ `DOWN` $\rightarrow$ `WEST`
7. `DROP KEYS` (Free up inventory slots)
8. `TAKE ROD` (Black rod with rusty star)
9. `WEST` (Fissure west)
10. `TAKE BIRD` (Fails if rod held! Drop rod, take bird, take rod!)
    - `DROP ROD` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE BIRD` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE ROD`
11. `WEST` (East bank of fissure)
12. `WAVE ROD` (A shimmering crystal bridge spans the fissure!)
13. `WEST` (Cross bridge to west side)
14. `TAKE DIAMONDS` (Treasure #1)
15. `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `UP` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` (Debris room)
16. `XYZZY` $\rightarrow$ `DROP DIAMONDS` $\rightarrow$ `XYZZY`

---

### Phase 2: Slaying the Snake & Gold Nugget
17. `WEST` $\rightarrow$ `DOWN` $\rightarrow$ `SOUTH` (Hall of Mists)
    *(Dwarf appears and throws axe; dodge it!)*
18. `TAKE AXE`
19. `SOUTH` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE GOLD` (Treasure #2) $\rightarrow$ `NORTH`
20. `EAST` (Hall of the Mountain King; giant green snake blocks way)
21. `DROP BIRD` (Bird attacks snake! Snake flees!)
22. `TAKE BIRD`
23. `SOUTH` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE JEWELRY` (Treasure #3) $\rightarrow$ `NORTH`
24. `WEST` $\rightarrow$ `WEST` $\rightarrow$ `UP` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` (Debris room)
25. `XYZZY` $\rightarrow$ `DROP GOLD` $\rightarrow$ `DROP JEWELRY` $\rightarrow$ `DROP AXE` $\rightarrow$ `XYZZY`

---

### Phase 3: The Silver Bars & Y2 Portal

### Phase 5: The Giant Clam & Pearl

### Phase 6: The Dragon, Persian Rug & Ming Vase
49. `SOUTH` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `NORTH` (Mirror canyon)
50. `NORTH` (Secret canyon)
51. `EAST` (Oriental room)

57. `TAKE RUG` (Treasure #11)
58. `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `Y2` $\rightarrow$ `PLUGH`
59. `DROP PILLOW`

### Phase 7: The Chasm Troll & The Golden Chain
62. `SOUTH` $\rightarrow$ `DOWN` $\rightarrow$ `SW` (Barren room; fierce cave bear chained to wall)
63. `FEED BEAR` (Give meat; bear becomes docile and friendly!)
64. `UNLOCK CHAIN` (Using brass keys)
65. `TAKE CHAIN` (Treasure #12)
66. `TAKE BEAR` (Bear follows loyally)
67. `WEST` (Chasm edge; ugly troll blocks bridge demanding treasure)
68. `THROW BEAR` (Bear terrifies the troll; troll flees into abyss!)
69. `EAST` $\rightarrow$ `Y2` $\rightarrow$ `PLUGH`
70. `DROP CHAIN` $\rightarrow$ `PLUGH`

---

### Phase 8: The Mazes, Pirate Chest & Spices
71. Retrieve `SPICES` (Treasure #13) in Chamber of Boulders.
72. Navigate Maze of Twisty Little Passages to Pirate's Lair.
73. `TAKE CHEST` (Treasure #14).
74. Retrieve `COINS` (Treasure #15) in West Hall of Mists.
75. `PLUGH` $\rightarrow$ Deposit all remaining treasures inside the building!

---

### Phase 9: The Endgame & Grandmaster Score
76. Once all 15 treasures are inside the building, the cave echoes with a rumbling roar:
    `"A sepulchral voice chimes: The cave is closing!"`
77. Teleported to the **Repository** (South-west chamber).
78. Do NOT panic! Examine the room: find black rod and dynamite.
79. `DROP ROD` $\rightarrow$ `TAKE DYNAMITE`
80. Move to adjoining alcove:
81. `BLAST` $\rightarrow$ The blast shatters the sealed door!
82. Step out into the sunlight:
    `"You scored 350 out of a possible 350 using X turns. To achieve the next higher rating would be a neat trick! Congratulations!!"`
