TREK — How to Play

Command Deep Space · BSD trek(6) spiritual successor

The Mission

You are captain of the USS Enterprise NCC-1701. The Federation is under attack. Klingon warships have infiltrated Federation space across an 8×8 galaxy of quadrants. Your mission: hunt down and destroy every Klingon before the stardate budget runs out — or the Federation falls.

The Screen

USS
ENTERPRISE
Your ship. Detailed federation cruiser sprite with saucer section, secondary hull, twin warp nacelles, and NCC-1701 registry. Cyan running lights. When shields are up, a translucent cyan bubble surrounds the ship.
K-SHIP
Klingon warship. Four types (see below). Each is drawn rotated so its bow faces the Enterprise. Red running lights and a red menacing glow.
★
BASE
Federation starbase. Gold cross marker. Dock adjacent to fully repair systems, refuel, and rearm.
☀ STAR
Star. Solid navigation obstacle. Torpedoes hitting a star are absorbed silently. Don't collide.

The HUD

Four panels are always visible:

Ship Status (top-left): shields / hull / energy / torpedoes as coloured bars. Bars turn amber below 50%, red below 25%. The arrow next to shields indicates up (↑) or down (↓).

Systems (top-right): status of your 8 subsystems. OK = green, WEAK = amber, DAMAGED = pulsing red. Damaged systems refuse to operate: e.g., damaged phasers can't fire.

Sector Contacts (right): what's in your current quadrant — klingons, starbases, stars — plus total klingons remaining and stardate budget.

Bridge Log (bottom-right): rolling event log — kills (cyan), hits taken (red), damage warnings (amber), general events (grey).

Commands

Type each command at the Command > prompt and press Enter. Most commands have a short alias.

phaser 500
Fire phasers with 500 energy at all Klingons in this quadrant. Damage split, attenuated by distance.
torpedo 3
Fire photon torpedo at bearing 3 (see clock below). Costs 50 energy + 1 torpedo. Hits first target on path.
move 3 2
Course 3 (bearing), warp factor 2. Consumes energy (≈ warp²×10) + stardate. Warp < 1 = impulse (within sector).
impulse 3
Short impulse move — bearing 3, warp 0.5. Cheap sector-level move.
srscan
Refresh short-range scan (current quadrant details).
lrscan
Long-range scan — reveal adjacent quadrants (fog-of-war on Galaxy Chart lifts).
damages
Print full subsystem damage report.
dock
Dock at adjacent starbase — full refuel, repair, rearm.
shields up
Raise shields (initial 500, costs 50 energy).
shields down
Lower shields.
shields 300
Transfer 300 energy from reserves into shields.
computer
Trajectory calculator (helper).
help
Show inline command reference.
quit
End mission (counts as loss).

Bearing — Clock Face Convention

Course and torpedo bearings use a 12-hour clock face. 0 (or 12) = East, 3 = North, 6 = West, 9 = South. Fractional bearings work (e.g. 1.5 = NE):

Views

Tactical (default): current quadrant combat scene — ships, weapons, stars, starbase. Galaxy Chart: 8×8 strategic map. Scanned quadrants show Klingon count; unscanned quadrants are darkened (fog of war). Switch views with the V key or the buttons in the top bezel.

Winning

  • ✓ Destroy every Klingon in the galaxy before your stardate budget expires.

Losing

  • ✗ Stardate budget runs out — Federation defeated.
  • ✗ Enterprise hull destroyed (hull ≤ 0).
  • ✗ Life support fails (system heavily damaged).
  • ✗ Energy reserves exhausted.

Walk-through: Your First Kill

1. Mission opens at a safe quadrant. Type lrscan to reveal adjacent quadrants and see which contain Klingons (red markers on Galaxy Chart).
2. Switch to Galaxy Chart with V. Note the direction to the nearest Klingon quadrant.
3. Warp there. Example: move 0 2 = course East, warp 2. Costs ~40 energy, ~2 stardates.
4. On arrival, raise shields: shields up. Then transfer more energy: shields 500.
5. Attack: phaser 400 to fire phasers, or torpedo 6 to launch a torpedo (bearing 6 = West).
6. Klingons return fire. Repeat attack until all destroyed. Bridge log shows kills.
7. Between fights: damages to check systems, dock at nearest starbase to fully repair.

Tips for Beginners

  • Always raise shields before entering a hostile quadrant.
  • Phasers hit everything in the quadrant, damage split by target count. Torpedoes hit one target on trajectory.
  • Save torpedoes for tough enemies (Battlecruisers, Super-Commanders).
  • Docking fully repairs systems and refills energy/torps/shields. Use it after tough fights.
  • Every command consumes stardate. Long routes waste time — plan with the Galaxy Chart.
  • If your hull < 40%, run to a starbase. Don't stay in a fight.
  • The Super-Commander (3% spawn) can one-shot a weakened Enterprise. Full shields + full torpedoes before engaging.
