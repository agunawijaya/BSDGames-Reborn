## 2. Historical Roguelike Literature & Archives

1. **Brouwer, Andries (1985):**
   - *Hack 1.0.3 Announcement and Sources*. USENET newsgroup `mod.sources`, December 1985.
2. **Stephenson, Mike & NetHack DevTeam (1987):**
   - *NetHack: The Next Generation of Hack*. USENET newsgroup `comp.sources.games`, July 1987.
3. **Craddock, David L. (2015):**
   - *Dungeon Hacks: How NetHack, Angband, and Other Roguelikes Changed Video Games*. Press Start Press. Canton, Ohio.
4. **Harris, John (2006–2010):**
   - *@Play: Exploring the Roguelike*. Game Developer / Gamasutra Column Series.
5. **Berlin Interpretation (2008):**
   - *General Definition of What a Roguelike Is*. International Roguelike Development Conference, Berlin, Germany.
