# `bcd` — Lineage

> Where `bcd` sits in the family tree of retro-format utilities.

---

## Direct Siblings (BSDGames)

- `ppt` — paper tape format display.
- `morse` — Morse code formatter.
- `banner` — ASCII banner printer.

## Genre Ancestors

- IBM punched-card equipment and Hollerith codes.
- Early data-processing visualisation tools.

## Modern Descendants

- SVG punch-card generators.
- Retro-computing art projects.
- Museum and educational demos about data storage history.
- QR-code-to-punch-card visualisers.

## Influence

`bcd` is a canonical example of "reformat modern data as obsolete media." It influenced later toys that render text as paper tape, floppy disks, or cassette tapes.
